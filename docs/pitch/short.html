Le Concierge

Every building gets a front desk: a face at the door you can just talk to, and a resident who answers from their pocket.

The problem: the intercom hasn’t changed in forty years. If you’re not standing next to it, the courier leaves or a stranger tailgates in. A 24-hour desk costs too much, so we built one.

Where we use Mistral

Voice AgentThe door is the agent: a live session per door, tools ask_resident, log_event, hang_up.
WorkflowsFour in Studio: visitor-arrival waits on a signal, nightly-digest on a schedule, plus parcels and CCTV.
Le ChonkLarge 4 for jobs that can wait: digest, parcel notices, CCTV review. 74 s at the door, so Small runs it.
VoxtralRealtime transcription and speech, in the agent and in the workflow, one voice.
Vibe + MCPA le-concierge MCP server: “who’s in?”, “any parcels?”, “anything odd on CCTV?”
