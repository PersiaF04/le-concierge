Le Concierge01 / 08

The problem

The intercom has not changed in forty years. It still needs you standing next to it.

19:42
A courier buzzes flat 12 with a parcel for Persia.
19:42
Persia is on the fourth floor, in the shower. The handset rings once.
19:43
Nobody answers. The courier tailgates the next resident in, or leaves.
19:44
A “sorry we missed you” card. A stranger in the lobby. No record of either.

Big buildings fix this with a 24-hour concierge desk. Most buildings can’t pay for one. We built the desk.

Le Concierge04 / 08

Where we use Mistral

PieceWhere and how
Voice AgentThe door is the agent: a live voice session per door, mistral-small on the turns, tools ask_resident, log_event, hang_up.
Workflowsvisitor-arrival waits on the resident-decision signal · parcel-arrival · nightly-digest on a 19:00 schedule · cctv-event. All visible in Studio.
Le ChonkLarge 4 does the jobs that can wait: the nightly digest, parcel notices, flagging suspicious CCTV. At full reasoning it took 74 s, too slow for someone on the step, so Small runs the door.
VoxtralRealtime transcription and TTS inside the voice agent; transcribe and speak the reply in visitor-arrival, one voice for both.
Vibe + MCP liveA le-concierge MCP server and skill: Vibe answers “who’s in?”, “any parcels?”, “anything odd on CCTV?”.
Le Concierge05 / 08

Privacy by design

A concierge remembers residents, not strangers.

Faces stay on the device

MediaPipe and face-api.js run in the browser at the door.

Stored only after “that was me”

Recognition is opt-in, one tap by the resident.

Visitors are never enrolled

The vision model describes a scene. It never identifies anyone.

No account, no password

Use the phone app anonymously, or leave details if you want them.
